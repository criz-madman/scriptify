Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Scriptify Studio operates on privacy-first design principles. We do not sell your personal data or index your proprietary project scripts.

1 Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information required to manage your account and deliver ExtendScript automation tools:

  • Account Identity: Your email address and authentication tokens via Google/Firebase Authentication or email login.
  • Wallet & Activity: Credit top-up transactions, receipt references, and tool generation counters.
  • Session State: Local browser storage preferences such as panel layout modes and UI theme configurations.

2 Confidentiality of Your After Effects Compositions

Your animations, layer names, kinetic expressions, and custom script toolbars are assembled directly inside your browser or synthesized through ephemeral API requests. We never harvest, store, or train AI models on your proprietary project files, client names, or creative motion workflows.

3 Payment Processing & Security

Financial transactions for studio credits are processed through certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (Stripe / Razorpay). Scriptify never accesses, intercepts, or retains raw credit card numbers or bank security codes on our database servers.

4 Cookies and Browser Storage

Scriptify strictly uses browser localStorage for active user authentication tokens and interface states. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies, data broker trackers, or intrusive analytics scripts.

5 Your Data Rights & Erasure Requests

Under GDPR, CCPA, and international data protection laws, you have the right to request a complete copy of your stored records, or to request the permanent deletion and erasure of your profile and history.
